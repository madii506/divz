Your keys, your position

DIVZ never holds your SOL, your coins or your keys. The server only builds and simulates the transaction. Your wallet signs it, and the LP tokens land in your own wallet, where they work anywhere PumpSwap does, including pump.fun.

If the price moves more than your limit before it lands, the whole transaction fails and nothing moves.

FAQ

Questions.

Where do the divs come from?

Every trade on a pump.fun pool pays a fee. Part of it stays inside the pool for the people who supply its liquidity. Add liquidity and you own a share of the pool, so you own that share of every fee it keeps.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Your position holds both SOL and the coin. If the coin's price falls, your position falls with it, partly offset by fees. If the coin goes to zero, so does the coin half. The receipt shows this at full size before you add.

Does DIVZ hold my funds?

No. DIVZ builds and simulates the transaction; your wallet signs it. The LP tokens go straight to your wallet, and you can withdraw on DIVZ, on pump.fun or anywhere PumpSwap works.

What does it cost?

The Solana network fee and the pool's normal trading fee on the part that gets swapped. Withdrawing is free apart from network fees.

Which pools can I add to?

Any pump.fun coin that has graduated to its PumpSwap pool paired with SOL. Coins still on the bonding curve have no pool yet. Paste any coin address in the search to look it up on-chain.

Why do some pools pay more?

Divs depend on how much trades through the pool compared with how big it is. A busy, small pool pays a higher rate. Pools above about 420 SOL market cap keep a bigger share of each fee for liquidity.

Are the daily rates guaranteed?

No. They are the last 24 hours of real trading. Volume changes every day, and fees fall when trading slows.

How do I take my money out?

Open the pool, choose Withdraw, pick how much, and get it back as SOL or as SOL plus the coin. It's one transaction your wallet signs.
